## Uzi v1.0 - first release

Uzi is an AI companion for Windows that lives on your desktop. She talks, listens, walks
around your screens, sits on a couch and plays guitar. Her AI runs locally on your PC with Ollama.

### How to install
1. Download **my-AI-companion-v1.0.zip** below (not the "Source code" files).
2. Extract it and double click **Install.bat**.
3. Answer the questions, wait for **ALL DONE**, then double click **Run.bat**.

Windows only.
Needs about 8 GB RAM (or 2-4 GB with the small model) and internet for the first install.
Webcam, phone page and PC control are off until you turn them on.

I used AI to help write some of the code, the installer and the README.
